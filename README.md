### I'm Shalini Raman 

I am a **Junior Research Fellow** in the [Functional Genomics Laboratory](https://web.iitd.ac.in/) at **IIT Delhi** (M.Sc. Biophysics, University of Delhi), building reproducible bioinformatics pipelines and deep learning frameworks for multi-scale transcriptomics and computational pathology.

---

###  Core Research & Engineering Focus
* **Alternative Splicing & Transcriptomics:** High-throughput RNA-seq processing, microexon inclusion quantification (`VAST-TOOLS`, `STAR`), and cross-tissue splicing dysregulation analysis.
* **Spatial Transcriptomics & Computational Pathology:** End-to-end inference of spatial gene expression from H&E whole-slide images (WSIs) using deep learning (`PyTorch`, `DeepSpot-M`, `Scanpy`, `Squidpy`, `OpenSeadragon`, `FastAPI`).
* **Protein Biophysics & Foundation Models:** Sequence/structure representation learning (`ESM`, `Ankh`) and weak non-covalent interaction profiling in protein secondary structures.
* **Scalable Pipeline Engineering:** Containerized, HPC-ready workflows engineered for Linux clusters using `SLURM`, `Docker`, `Conda`, `Python`, and `R`.
